Biography

I am a Research Fellow at the School of Computing, National University of Singapore (NUS). I received both my M.S. and Ph.D. degrees from the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

My research lies at the intersection of computer architecture and systems security, with a focus on the security and reliability of modern processors. I am interested in practical and scalable approaches to processor verification, hardware vulnerability discovery, side-channel security, and hardware-assisted memory safety.

Research interests

  • Processor security and verification
  • Hardware vulnerability discovery
  • Side-channel security
  • Hardware-assisted memory safety
